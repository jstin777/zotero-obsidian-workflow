# Third-party software

The repository's original Python/JavaScript, documentation and synthetic examples are MIT licensed. The Scite snapshot helper and QMD installer/lock were extracted from the maintainer's own workflow and adapted for this template. Third-party applications, libraries, model weights and plugins are installed separately; this repository does not relicense them.
